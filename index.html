What we are doing

Most serious immune disease is a problem of control rather than strength. The immune system attacks a transplanted organ, a donor graft, or the body itself, and the standard answer is to suppress it broadly — for years, at real cost to the patient.

Tutela is developing a different approach: cell therapies built from regulatory T cells, the population whose natural job is to hold immune responses in check. Our platform produces them from banked material rather than from each individual patient, which is what makes a therapy rather than a bespoke procedure.

Where we are aiming

  • TransplantationReducing the lifelong immunosuppression that follows solid-organ transplant.
  • Graft-versus-host diseasePreventing and treating GvHD in patients receiving stem-cell transplants.
  • OncologyEarlier-stage work applying engineered cells to tumours that current therapies reach poorly.

Why Shanghai

China performs the second-largest number of transplants in the world and a substantial share of global stem-cell transplants, with clinical need concentrated in exactly the settings our work addresses.

The company is registered in the Pudong free trade zone, within the Zhangjiang life science cluster, and works alongside Chinese academic and clinical partners.

Who we are

A small founding team of scientists and operators, with an academic institutional partner and clinical collaborators in Shanghai. We are in the pre-clinical stage and not yet recruiting broadly.
